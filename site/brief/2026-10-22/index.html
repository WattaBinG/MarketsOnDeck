Nasdaq Futures Tumble 1.93% as Tesla Slides and Brent Hits $112 — the AI Bill Comes Due

Drafted premarket Thursday, October 22, before the opening bell, after Wednesday’s close.

October 22, 2026Morning Brief

Drafted premarket Thursday, October 22, before the opening bell, after Wednesday’s close.

S&P 500 -1.10% · Nasdaq 100 -1.93% · Dow -0.69% — overnight futures vs Wednesday's closes (S&P 500 7,757.17, Nasdaq Composite 26,702.86, Dow 52,856.09). Percents: Reuters futures, 4:56 AM ET; index closes: WSJ, Oct 21.

The setup — beats were not enough. Meta topped estimates on $64.60 billion of revenue and Tesla beat on both lines — $0.48 adjusted EPS vs $0.43 expected, $28.05 billion of revenue vs $27.88 billion expected (LSEG via CNBC) — and futures still rolled over. Meta created a new class of non-voting shares to help fund its AI push, and Tesla's GAAP net income fell 43% to $787 million (Reuters; Morningstar/Dow Jones Newswires). The question flipped overnight from what AI earns to what AI costs.

The Mag 7 — every name red before the bell. In Robinhood extended hours (~7:17 AM ET), Tesla traded $373.30 (-3.43%) vs its $386.56 close, Meta $682.29 (-2.31%), Alphabet $327.41 (-2.17%), Nvidia $217.47 (-1.98%), Amazon $257.71 (-1.77%), Microsoft $528.30 (-0.85%) and Apple $343.05 (-0.29%). Chips led the slide: Lam Research -7.31% in extended hours after soft guidance, and SpaceX $155.89 (-2.13%) a day after its first profit as a public company.

Oil — the second headline. Brent crude climbed more than 3% to $112 a barrel, near a five-month high, after Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia stoked supply fears (Reuters). A flight to safety pulled the 10-year Treasury yield down to around 5.085% (Reuters). Crypto went risk-off with it: Bitcoin $81,881.83 (-1.18%) vs its session open and Ether $2,401.15 (-1.70%) (Robinhood crypto quotes, ~7:17 AM ET).

Earnings — defense and staples carried the morning. Lockheed Martin beat ($7.97 vs $7.51 estimated), Valero crushed it ($23.90 vs $21.20), Procter & Gamble beat ($2.00 vs $1.89), Honeywell beat ($2.27 vs $2.17) and American Airlines lost less than feared (-$0.28 vs -$0.35); Spotify missed ($2.85 vs $3.11) (Robinhood earnings calendar, Oct 22).

What to watch today:

  • Thu Oct 22, 8:30 AM ET — initial jobless claims (consensus 200K, prior 199K)
  • Thu Oct 22, after the close — Western Digital, Newmont and Deckers report
  • All session — whether Brent holds over $112 and turns a tech story into an inflation story
