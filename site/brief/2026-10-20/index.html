October 20, 2026Morning Brief

Drafted premarket Tuesday, October 20, before the opening bell, after Monday’s rebound.

Monday's rebound lifted the Nasdaq 100 ETF 1.20% — this morning Brent crude sits at $110.07, a five-month high, Nasdaq 100 futures are down 0.46% on AI debt fears, and tonight Netflix and SpaceX deliver the first verdicts of earnings week.

Futures are mixed-to-lower after Monday's rebound. As of 5:34 AM ET (TipRanks): Nasdaq 100 futures -0.46%, S&P 500 futures -0.07% and Dow futures +0.22%. The drag is the AI complex again — debt-funded AI buildout worries pushed CoreWeave to a 52-week low (TipRanks), and in Robinhood extended-hours prints (~7:19 AM ET) CRWV traded $58.35 (-4.52% vs its Monday close), MU $880.68 (-2.72%), INTC $90.35 (-1.76%), NVDA $215.80 (-0.59%) and QQQ $714.12 (-0.58%). Oil is the other headwind: Brent crude futures rose 0.47% to $110.07 a barrel by 0628 GMT, hovering near a five-month high ($110.70 on Monday, the highest since May 20) as Houthi attacks on Saudi infrastructure kept a geopolitical risk premium in the market (Reuters); WTI November futures were $104.03 (+0.36%). Monday's official closes (Robinhood equity quotes) set the bar the session has to defend: SPY $766.54 (+0.43%), QQQ $718.32 (+1.20%) and DIA $518.99 (-0.02%). The Mag 7 mostly bounced — AMZN $262.44 (+4.97%), TSLA $368.09 (+3.77%), NVDA $217.09 (+1.38%), GOOGL $344.20 (+1.06%) and MSFT $533.61 (+0.86%) — while META $704.95 (-0.62%) and AAPL $342.97 (-0.14%) lagged. Crypto faded with risk: BTC mark $81,411.87 (-0.76% vs its session open) and ETH mark $2,381.38 (-1.28%), Robinhood crypto quotes at 7:19 AM ET.

This morning's earnings are a clean sweep of beats so far (Robinhood earnings calendar, Oct 20, pre-open reports): Northrop Grumman (NOC) $7.89 vs a $7.19 estimate, RTX $2.26 vs $1.78, Hasbro (HAS) $2.90 vs $1.85, GE Aerospace (GE) $2.25 vs $2.01, GM $3.70 vs $3.61, 3M (MMM) $2.50 vs $2.39, Equifax (EFX) $2.26 vs $2.20 and Halliburton (HAL) $0.60 vs $0.58. Extended-hours trade is not rewarding all of them — GM printed $78.92 (-4.59% vs its $82.72 Monday close) and GE $306.71 (-1.48%) in Robinhood extended-hours prints — so guidance, not the headline EPS, is doing the talking. After today's close comes the week's first main event: Netflix (NFLX, est $0.82; the stock is down about 26% year-to-date into the print, TipRanks) and SpaceX (SPCX, est $0.16), plus United Airlines (UAL, est $2.59), Intuitive Surgical (ISRG, est $2.54), Capital One (COF, est $5.49) and Chubb (CB, est $6.74). Wednesday after the close: Meta (est $5.89) and Tesla (est $0.40). Levels to watch: QQQ closed $718.32 with extended trade at $714.12 — holding Monday's close is the rebound's first test; NVDA closed $217.09, extended $215.80, with Friday's $214.13 close below that; Brent at $110.07 — a push through Monday's $110.70 high adds pressure to everything rate- and fuel-sensitive. Watch today: whether the chip slide (CRWV, MU, INTC all red premarket) drags QQQ under its Monday close in the first hour, and whether Brent holds above $110 into the Netflix and SpaceX prints after the close.
