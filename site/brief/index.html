August 29, 2026

The One Thing

NVDA closed up 8.74% to $227.98 on nearly 293M shares traded — one of the most actively traded names in the entire market, not just a small mover. This is a name I actually hold.

Worth Watching

  • NVDA — that volume spike is the story. Whatever moved it is going to shape sentiment in every other chip name today, including INTC, also up notably (+4.36%).
  • Semiconductors broadly — INTC's move alongside NVDA suggests this isn't a single-stock story.
  • Crypto stayed open all weekend as usual, low drama by comparison.

Why This Matters

A single-name 8%+ move on this much volume usually isn't noise — it's either a real catalyst or a sector-wide rotation dragging the whole group with it. Today's the day to know which before reacting to it.
