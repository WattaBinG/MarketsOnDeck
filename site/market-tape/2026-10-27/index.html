Boeing Plunged 10.70% and the Dow Still Finished Green — Broadcom's $63 Billion Anthropic Deal Pushed the Nasdaq-100 Up 1.29%

First published October 27, 2026, 5:12 PM ET · All After Market Tape entries →

Share X Facebook LinkedIn
October 27, 2026After Market Tape

Boeing fell 10.70% on Tuesday and the Dow still finished fractionally higher, because tech carried the tape again: Broadcom jumped 4.04% on a $63 billion Anthropic accelerator deal (AP), Tesla added 4.04% for a second straight day, and the Nasdaq-100 rose 1.29% to 29,651.44 the day before the Fed decides.

Recap

Tech carried the tape for a second straight day on Tuesday. At the official closes (Robinhood), SPY rose 0.57% to $769.72, QQQ rose 1.27% to $721.24 and DIA rose 0.06% to $523.26. The S&P 500 index printed 7,722.60 (+0.60% vs Monday's 7,676.77 close) and the Nasdaq-100 printed 29,651.44 (+1.29%), with the VIX down 4.73% at 17.52 (Robinhood index quotes). The Dow finished fractionally higher even as Boeing fell 10.70% to $168.16 on its earnings day — a drop Investopedia said limited the blue-chip index's gains. Chips led the advance: the iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) rose 3.93% to $511.18, Broadcom rose 4.04% to $342.46 after announcing a $63 billion funding deal giving Anthropic access to its XPU accelerators and networking solutions (AP), and memory names followed South Korean trading higher — Western Digital rose 6.90% to $380.69, SanDisk rose 3.87% to $1,304.22, Seagate rose 3.73% to $668.21 and Micron rose 2.82% to $929.17 (Robinhood closes; Investopedia). Oil fell for a second day: WTI crude pulled back 1.9% to below $100 a barrel and Brent declined 1.7% to below $114 around 4 PM ET, while the 10-year Treasury yield was nearly flat near 4.99% (Investopedia). Bitcoin reclaimed the $80,000 line — around $80,600 in late trading (Investopedia), with Robinhood's evening mark at $80,571.51.

Mag 7 on Tuesday, with Tesla out front for a second straight day. Source: Robinhood official closes, October 27, 2026.

The Mag 7 split again on Tuesday (Robinhood official closes): Tesla $407.46 (+4.04%) paced the group for a second straight day after Monday's 5.23% jump. Amazon rose 1.63% to $253.16, Alphabet rose 1.19% to $320.56 and Apple rose 0.92% to $347.08. Nvidia rose 0.89% to $217.92 after saying it will invest $3 billion in Meta's equity offering (Investopedia). Meta fell 1.66% to $620.12 — a second straight down day on its upsized $97.5 billion AI fundraising — and Microsoft slipped 0.19% to $531.60 ahead of its Wednesday report. The Roundhill MAGS ETF, which tracks the seven, finished 1% higher (Investopedia). Elsewhere, post-earnings movers ran hot: JetBlue soared 18.78% to $2.81, SoFi surged 13.38% after its morning earnings beat ($0.18 vs $0.17 estimated, Robinhood earnings calendar), UPS gained 5.79% to $106.13 and Royal Caribbean gained 5.47% to $295.95, while Sherwin-Williams fell 2.75% to $325.95 after its profit miss, Coca-Cola fell 1.64% to $91.18 and Corning slipped 1.04% to $126.41 (Robinhood closes; Investopedia). Neoclouds ripped with the chips: Nebius rose 11.22% to $170.47, CoreWeave rose 7.90% to $59.55 and Super Micro rose 7.33% to $35.09 (Robinhood closes).

Watch tomorrow: the Fed decision on Wednesday (markets price just a 7% chance of a hike this week, CME FedWatch via Investopedia), Alphabet and Microsoft earnings after Wednesday's close with Amazon on Thursday, tonight's after-close reactions from Visa, Robinhood and Seagate, and whether Bitcoin can hold $80,000.
