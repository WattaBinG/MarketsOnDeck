After Market Tape — Tesla's calls get sold out, Home Depot's bet doubles down at a cheaper strike, and SpaceX gets trimmed into a successful Crew-13 launch

SPY closed today at $764.39, up 0.23% from Wednesday's $762.63 — a quiet headline number for the first session of October, with a clearer driver underneath it than the index move alone suggests. QQQ gained 0.37% to $742.51, DIA added 0.03% to $508.71, and small-cap IWM led the benchmarks at $279.34, up 0.52%. Per Yahoo Finance's coverage of the session, the advance traced to Treasury yields retreating from multiyear highs, a move that gave chip stocks room to run.

Why this matters: today closed out three separate bets rather than opening new uncertainty — the TSLA calls, the old HD $300 calls, and the second Carnival call all came off the book, two for a loss and one for a gain — while the Home Depot options position got bigger and cheaper on the same day the stock kept falling, a clearer bet that today's price is temporary than yesterday's smaller add was. None of that is resolved: Tesla's actual Q3 delivery number lands tomorrow, Friday, October 2, the same morning the now-closed TSLA and HD $300 contracts would have expired anyway — meaning today's exits were decided before the number everyone's been waiting on, not after it.
