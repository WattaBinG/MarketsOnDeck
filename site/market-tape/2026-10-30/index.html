Nasdaq-100 Falls 3.46% as Intel's Weak Outlook Deepens the Tech Sell-Off

First published October 30, 2026, 5:24 PM ET · All After Market Tape entries →

Share X Facebook LinkedIn
October 30, 2026 | After Market Tape

MarketsOnDeck | After-Market Tape | Friday, October 30, 2026

The S&P 500 finished Friday at 7,580.07, down 1.82%, while the Nasdaq-100 fell 3.46% to 28,384.85. The Dow fell 1.09% to 51,770.31. Index closes were cross-checked against Yahoo Finance daily data.

Technology stocks led the sell-off after disappointing reports from Intel and Amazon. CNBC reported that concern about AI spending and returns weighed on the sector, while Intel's outlook also reflected weakness in the PC market.

Intel's forecast falls short

Intel forecast fourth-quarter revenue of $15 billion to $16 billion and adjusted earnings of 25 cents a share. Analysts surveyed by LSEG expected $17.1 billion and 43 cents, according to CNBC. The company said it expects the PC slowdown to continue through at least the first half of next year.

What to watch next week

Watch whether weakness remains concentrated in technology or spreads across the broader market. Intel's next test is how it manages the PC slowdown alongside demand for AI infrastructure. No stock or ETF closing prices are included in this edition.
