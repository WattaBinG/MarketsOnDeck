Anthropic Just Filed to Go Public, and Tesla Ripped 5.23% on a $450 Target as the S&P 500 Climbed 0.70%

First published October 26, 2026, 5:16 PM ET · All After Market Tape entries →

Share X Facebook LinkedIn
October 26, 2026After Market Tape

Anthropic filed its S-1 to go public and tech caught a bid on day one of the biggest earnings week of the year: Tesla jumped 5.23% on a Barclays upgrade to a $450 target, Nvidia rose 2.38%, and the S&P 500 closed up 0.70% at 7,676.77 while oil cooled.

Recap

The relief rally broadened on Monday. At the official closes (Robinhood), SPY rose 0.70% to $765.29, QQQ rose 1.10% to $712.12 and DIA rose 0.31% to $522.97. The S&P 500 index printed 7,676.77 (+0.70% vs Friday's 7,623.68 close) and the Nasdaq-100 printed 29,273.40 (+1.09%), with the VIX down at 18.39 from 19.45 on Friday (Robinhood index quotes; CNBC confirmed the S&P close). Tech led after Anthropic filed its S-1 paperwork to go public — revenue of $16.3 billion and a net loss of $80.7 billion in the six months through June, versus $1.2 billion and $13.0 billion a year earlier (S-1 via StockTitan; CNBC). Oil kept cooling: WTI crude fell just under 1% to around $102.35 a barrel and the 10-year Treasury yield was nearly flat at 4.99% (Investor's Business Daily). Surgery Partners soared 47.62% to $23.25 after confirming it will be acquired for approximately $5.7 billion (Robinhood close; WSJ). Brazil-linked names went the other way after President Lula won a fourth term on Sunday: the iShares Brazil ETF (EWZ) fell 19.83% to $34.15 and MercadoLibre fell 7.81% to $1,668.72 (Robinhood closes; CNBC).

Mag 7 on Monday, with Tesla out front on a Barclays upgrade. Source: Robinhood official closes, October 26, 2026.

The Mag 7 split on Monday (Robinhood official closes): Tesla $391.69 (+5.23%) led the group after Barclays analyst Dan Levy upgraded the stock to buy from hold and raised his target to $450 from $370 (CNBC; The Motley Fool). Nvidia rose 2.38% to $215.99, Microsoft rose 1.57% to $532.75 and Amazon rose 0.41% to $249.01. Meta fell 1.89% to $630.47 — the group's laggard on the day its filings detailed a roughly $95 billion financing plan for AI infrastructure, about $35 billion of underwritten offerings plus a planned $60 billion at-the-market program (SEC filing) — while Apple fell 1.08% to $343.98 and Alphabet fell 1.04% to $316.89. Elsewhere in tech, Arm rose 6.19% to $223.35 and Cisco rose 3.53% to $110.26, while Merck fell 3.22% to $140.59 as the Dow's worst laggard (Robinhood closes; Investor's Business Daily). Micron rose 2.98% to $903.70 and Broadcom rose 2.37% to $329.29 (Robinhood closes).

Watch tomorrow: whether the Anthropic-led tech bid and Tesla's 5.23% jump hold into Tuesday, the after-close reactions from Cadence Design Systems and Nucor, and the run into Wednesday's Fed decision and Alphabet and Microsoft earnings.
