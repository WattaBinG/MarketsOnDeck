Who's Behind This

About MarketsOnDeck

MarketsOnDeck is run by one person: me, Keith. This is my real brokerage account, documented in public — not a persona, not a fund, not a signals service.

Why This Exists

Most trading content online falls into one of two buckets: unverifiable "gains screenshot" flexing, or the loss-porn that fills up trading subreddits with no context and no follow-through. Neither is honest documentation. I wanted to try the third option — post the real account, wins and losses both, in a way that's actually checkable instead of just asserted.

How the Graded-Call System Works

The idea driving this site is simple: a trade call only means something if it was written down before the outcome was known. So every call gets logged the moment it's made — ticker, thesis, confidence, timeframe — in a record that isn't edited after the fact. Grading happens afterward, in a separate log. A bad call can't quietly disappear, and a good call can't be quietly backdated.

Episode recaps and the Morning Brief pull from this same real account — not a demo account, not a hypothetical portfolio.

What "Verified" Actually Means Here

Every dollar figure published on this site is checked against the source data before it goes live — usually by more than one independent pass. When two numbers disagree, that gets resolved and disclosed, not quietly rounded away. If the underlying trade log is incomplete for a given period (it happens — this is a real account, maintained by a real person, not an automated feed), that gets stated plainly rather than papered over.
