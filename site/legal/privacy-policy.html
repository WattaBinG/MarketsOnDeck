Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026
Before you rely on this: this policy accurately describes how MarketsOnDeck operates today. It is not a substitute for review by a qualified attorney, especially once analytics or email capture are added. Update this page whenever the site's actual data-collecting tools change.

Who We Are

MarketsOnDeck ("we," "us," "our") operates this website. Questions: hello@marketsondeck.net.

An Important Distinction: The Trading Data You See vs. Your Data

This site publishes real trade data from the site owner's own personal brokerage account, chosen for publication by the owner. That is the owner's own financial data, published deliberately as content — it is not collected from you, the visitor, and has nothing to do with your personal information. This section is about your data as a visitor to the site.

What This Site Currently Collects From Visitors

As of the effective date above, MarketsOnDeck does not run third-party analytics, does not require account creation, and does not collect any visitor financial information. Standard web server logs maintained by our hosting provider may automatically record technical information (IP address, browser type, pages requested) consistent with that provider's own privacy practices — we do not separately access or analyze this for individual visitor tracking.

Affiliate tracking links: when you click a link to a partner platform (Robinhood, Webull, TradingView, and others named in our Affiliate Disclosure), that link passes through the affiliate network's tracking system before reaching the partner's site, which may set a tracking cookie on the partner's domain. We do not control this directly — it's governed by that network's and partner's own privacy policies.

If we add analytics, email capture, or advertising in the future, this section will be updated to name the specific tool before it goes live.

Cookies

This site itself does not set first-party tracking cookies as of the effective date above. Cookies encountered after clicking an outbound affiliate link are set by that third party, not by MarketsOnDeck.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

EU/EEA visitors have rights to access, correct, delete, and restrict processing of any personal data we hold — contact us to exercise these. California residents have rights to know, delete, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; MarketsOnDeck does not sell personal information. Submit requests to hello@marketsondeck.net.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
