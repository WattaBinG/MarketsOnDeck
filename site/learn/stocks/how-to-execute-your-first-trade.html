Stocks 101

How to execute your first trade

The mechanical steps from account to order. What to buy is your decision, not this guide's.

On this page
  1. 1. Open a brokerage account
  2. 2. Fund the account
  3. 3. Pick what you want to research
  4. 4. Choose a quantity and an order type
  5. 5. Review and submit
  6. 6. Afterward

This walks through the mechanics only: how an account gets set up and how an order works. It does not tell you what to buy.

1. Open a brokerage account

Pick a broker, then provide your identity details, employment info and a funding source. Approval can take minutes to a few days. Brokers differ on fees, tools and extended-hours access, so compare a couple.

2. Fund the account

Link a bank account and transfer money. Transfers can take a few business days to be fully available. Only fund an account with money you can afford to lose.

3. Pick what you want to research

Decide for yourself, using your own research. Know what the company does, how it makes money and what could go wrong.

4. Choose a quantity and an order type

Enter the ticker, then buy or sell, quantity and order type. For a first trade many people use a limit order so they control the price. See the order types article.

5. Review and submit

Check the ticker, share count, order type and estimated cost on the review screen. Then submit. A filled order shows up in your positions.

6. Afterward

Note why you bought and what would make you sell. Decide your exit before you enter, so that emotions do not make the decision later.
