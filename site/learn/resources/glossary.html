Glossary

35 terms. Type to filter.

Ask
The lowest price a seller will accept right now.
Assignment
When an option seller is required to fulfill the contract after the buyer exercises.
At the money
An option whose strike is at or very near the stock price.
Bid
The highest price a buyer will pay right now.
Bid-ask spread
The gap between the bid and the ask. A cost of trading.
Call option
A contract giving the buyer the right to buy 100 shares at the strike price by expiration.
Candlestick
A chart bar showing open, high, low and close for a period.
Delta
How much an option's price moves per $1 move in the stock.
Dividend
A cash payment some companies make to shareholders.
EMA
Exponential moving average. An average that weights recent prices more.
ETF
Exchange-traded fund. A basket of assets that trades like a stock.
Expiration
The date an option contract ends.
Extended hours
Trading before the 9:30 AM open or after the 4:00 PM close, Eastern.
Gamma
How fast an option's delta changes as the stock moves.
Implied volatility (IV)
The market's estimate of future movement, built into option prices.
In the money
A call with the stock above the strike, or a put with the stock below it.
Intrinsic value
How far an option is in the money.
Limit order
An order that only fills at your price or better.
Liquidity
How easily you can trade without moving the price.
Margin
Borrowing from a broker, or the collateral required for certain trades.
Market cap
Share price times shares outstanding. The market's value of a company.
Market order
An order that fills now at the best available price.
Open interest
The number of option contracts outstanding.
Out of the money
A call with the stock below the strike, or a put with the stock above it.
Premium
The price of an option contract, quoted per share.
Put option
A contract giving the buyer the right to sell 100 shares at the strike price by expiration.
RSI
Relative strength index. A 0 to 100 momentum gauge, usually 14 periods.
SMA
Simple moving average. The plain average of closing prices over a set period.
Stop-loss order
An order that becomes a market order when a trigger price is hit.
Strike price
The price at which an option can be exercised.
Support and resistance
Price levels where a stock has tended to pause or reverse.
Theta
How much an option loses in value each day from time passing.
Time value
The part of an option's premium that is not intrinsic value.
Vega
How much an option's price changes when implied volatility changes.
Volume
The number of shares traded in a period.
