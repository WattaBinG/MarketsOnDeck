# MARKETSONDECK - STRATEGIC SITE BRIEF

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
- **Name:** MarketsOnDeck
- **Tagline:** Real Calls, Graded in Public — Wins and Losses Both.
- **Core Positioning:** A trading/markets publisher built around one mechanic nobody else in this niche does: every call is logged before the outcome is known, in an immutable database, and graded afterward without the ability to quietly delete the losers. The anti-Reddit-loss-porn, anti-guru-signals-service account.

## 2. Target Audience (Ideal Customer Profile)
- **Who They Are:** Retail traders and investors — options traders, swing traders, crypto-curious, prediction-market bettors — who are tired of unverifiable "look at my gains" content and want to follow someone who shows their actual track record, good and bad.
- **Core Frustrations:**
  - Trading content that only ever shows winners, or posts results after the fact with no way to verify timing.
  - Paid "signals" services and gurus with no accountability when calls go wrong.
  - Difficulty telling a real trader from an affiliate-link farm dressed up as one.
- **Desired Outcome:** A trustworthy daily read, a real track record they can audit themselves, and honest platform/tool comparisons from someone who's actually used them to trade real money.

## 3. The 4 Content Pillars
1. **Graded Trade Calls:** The core differentiator. Every call logged via Markets on Deck OS (the hypothesis tracker) before the outcome is known; resolved and graded afterward in a separate, append-only record.
2. **Morning Brief:** Daily, short, written fresh every trading day — what's actually worth watching, not a wall of regurgitated headlines.
3. **Platform & Tool Reviews:** Brokerages, charting tools, and algo platforms tested with real money and real usage — Robinhood, Webull, Moomoo, TradingView, and others as they're actually used, not as press-released.
4. **Prediction Markets & Alt-Markets:** Kalshi and similar regulated event-contract platforms — a natural extension given the audience overlap between trading, sports, and prediction markets.

## 4. Revenue Engine
- **Primary:** CPA (cost-per-acquisition) bounties from brokerage/exchange referral programs — Robinhood ($5/lead + $20/funded account via Impact), Webull ($20-$70/funded account via Impact), Moomoo (CPA, cash/stock rewards), Kalshi (~$25/first deposit + revshare, program not publicly self-serve — requires direct outreach).
- **Secondary:** Recurring commission from trading tools — TradingView (30% recurring, 90-day cookie) is the standout genuine recurring program in this space; prioritize accordingly since it behaves like AgenticToolbox's revenue model while the brokerage CPA deals don't.
- **Unconfirmed / Do Not Commit Yet:** Coinbase (conflicting commission reports — verify directly), StockAlgos (no confirmed public affiliate program found).
- **Tertiary (Future):** Premium/paid tier for the full graded-call database and historical accuracy stats once the free track record has built real audience trust.

## 5. Why the Hypothesis Tracker Matters Here (Not Just a Database)
Most trading-content sites fail the credibility test the moment a skeptical reader asks "how do I know you actually called that before it happened?" Markets on Deck OS answers that structurally, not with a promise: the call table is written-once, the resolution table is separate and append-only, and both can be shown to a reader on request. This is the single biggest differentiator in the plan — everything else (Morning Brief, reviews, prediction markets) is standard content-site execution, but the graded-call mechanic is the actual moat.
