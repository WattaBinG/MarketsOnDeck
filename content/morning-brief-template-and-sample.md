# Morning Brief — Template + Real Sample

## The Format (per CLAUDE.md §4 — keep this short, it's a daily habit-read not an article)

1. **Overnight/futures context** — one or two sentences, what actually moved while markets were closed
2. **2-3 things worth watching today** — named, specific, no generic "watch the market" filler
3. **Any graded-call updates due to resolve** — pulled from the Markets on Deck OS log once that's live; skip this section until then
4. **One plain-language "why this matters" close** — ties it back to something a retail trader/investor actually cares about

FTC + investment-advice disclaimer goes at the top of every single brief, no exceptions — this is the highest-frequency content on the site, which means it's also the highest-frequency compliance surface.

## Under the Hood (Internal Note — do not publish)

This sample deliberately ties the brief back to your own actual NVDA position — that's the format's real advantage over a generic market newsletter: you're not summarizing headlines, you're the person whose own account is exposed to what you're writing about. Worth leaning into that connection explicitly in the real daily version ("this is a name I'm actually holding") rather than writing at arm's length like a wire-service recap.

**Reality check on the daily commitment:** a genuinely daily brief is a real ongoing production commitment, not a one-time build — every trading day, indefinitely. Worth deciding up front whether this is truly daily, or whether "most trading days" with an honest occasional skip is the more sustainable version before the format becomes a source of guilt instead of a habit.
